5. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users to provide, maintain, and optimize our services:

5.1 Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

  • Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender;
  • Mobile Phone Number, Email Address;
  • Permanent and Delivery Addresses (including Pincode);
  • Government-issued identification details (e.g., PAN, Aadhaar number where voluntarily provided or required for KYC verification under RBI/AML rules);
  • Financial Details: Bank account details, UPI ID, VPA, and masked credit/debit card metadata (processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways).

5.2 Device and Technical Data

  • IP Address, Device ID, IMEI, MAC Address;
  • Operating System, Browser Type, App Version, Platform Build Details;
  • Mobile Network Operator, SIM Serial Details (for SMS-based device binding and UPI authentication);
  • Push Notification Tokens, Crash Logs, and Performance Telemetry.

5.3 Financial, Transactional, and Usage Data

  • Transaction History: Value, timestamp, merchant details, payment modes, success/failure status;
  • Reward Logs: KAMALO Coins earned, burnt, pending, or expired;
  • Interaction Logs: Features clicked, products viewed, cart additions, search queries, session duration.

5.4 SMS and Communication Data (Conditional & Consent-Based)

Where explicit permission is granted, We may process financial transaction SMS messages sent by banks or billers to automatically detect bills, verify transactions, and streamline reward distribution. We do not read personal or non-financial SMS messages.

5.5 Location Data

  • Approximate location derived from IP address or network cells;
  • Precise GPS Location (collected only with explicit in-app permission) to show local merchant deals, facilitate geo-fenced rewards, or satisfy fraud prevention rules.

6. How We Collect Information

We collect data through the following methods:

  • Direct Interactions: Information provided by You during account registration, waiting list sign-ups, profile updates, support requests, or survey forms.
  • Automated Technologies: Technical logs, server logs, cookies, SDKs, and local storage collected automatically when You navigate the Platform.
  • Third-Party Partners: Identity verification partners, NPCI/UPI ecosystem partners, payment aggregators, credit bureaus, and marketing analytics providers.

7. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is collected and processed for the following purposes:

  • Core Service Delivery: To setup Your account, process payments, facilitate merchant transactions, and execute bill recharges.
  • Reward Program Administration: To calculate, credit, track, and allow redemption of KAMALO Coins and promotional cashbacks.
  • KYC & Identity Verification: To verify Your identity, conduct fraud risk assessments, and comply with statutory KYC/AML obligations.
  • Communication & Notifications: To send transactional alerts, OTPs, order updates, policy updates, and promotional communications (where opted-in).
  • Platform Security & Operations: To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraud, unauthorized transactions, cyber threats, and technical errors.
  • Analytics & Improvement: To evaluate feature performance, run A/B tests, customize user experience, and refine our machine learning algorithms.

  • Legal Enforcement & Regulatory Compliance: When required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental authority to investigate illegal activities, fraud, or threats to national security.
  • Corporate Restructuring: In connection with any merger, acquisition, asset sale, or restructuring, provided the receiving entity agrees to honor this Privacy Policy.

11. Data Security Measures

We implement robust technical, operational, and managerial security controls to protect Your data against loss, misuse, unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Security measures include:

  • End-to-end encryption of sensitive data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256);

  • Regular Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT);

While We strive to protect Your data, no digital transmission or storage system is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials and OTPs.

Upon expiry of the retention period or upon valid receipt of an account deletion request, We securely delete, anonymize, or de-identify your Personal Data, retaining only aggregated, non-identifiable data for analytical purposes.
