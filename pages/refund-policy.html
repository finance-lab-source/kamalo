1. Scope & Applicability

This Refund and Cancellation Policy governs the terms under which cancellations, refunds, reversals, and transaction adjustments are processed for services, bill payments, digital recharges, merchant vouchers, and rewards on the KAMALO Platform operated by Kamal Intellect Private Limited.

2. General Cancellation Rules

  • Finality of User Actions: Transactions initiated on the Platform (such as utility bill payments, mobile recharges, merchant gift card purchases, and peer-to-peer transfers) are processed instantly through automated payment networks. Once submitted, cancellations by the user are generally not permitted.
  • System-Initiated Cancellations: KAMALO reserves the right to cancel any order or transaction if:
    • The transaction is flagged as suspicious or fraudulent by automated risk checks;
    • The inventory/voucher is unavailable from the merchant;
    • Technical failure prevents processing by payment partners.

3. Marketplace & Merchant Orders

  • Direct Merchant Responsibility: For goods or services ordered from third-party merchants via KAMALO, cancellation rules are governed by the respective merchant's cancellation policy.
  • Defective Products/Services: If a product or merchant service is defective, missing, or materially different from listed specifications, Users must raise a ticket within 48 hours of delivery via kamaloapp09@gmail.com or the in-app support portal.

4. Recharges, Bill Payments & Digital Services

  • Successful Transactions: Mobile recharges, DTH, electricity, water, gas, and broadband bill payments successfully processed and confirmed by the biller/operator cannot be cancelled or refunded.
  • Wrong Account / Number Entered: KAMALO is not responsible for refunds if the user enters incorrect account numbers, consumer IDs, or mobile numbers.
  • Biller Pending Status: If a bill payment status shows "Pending", funds remain blocked while the biller processes the request. If the biller rejects the payment, a refund will automatically be triggered.

5. Gift Cards & Vouchers

Purchases of digital gift cards, brand vouchers, and instant coupon codes are non-refundable and non-exchangeable once the code or digital voucher link has been generated and delivered to the user.

6. Failed, Pending or Duplicate Payment Transactions

  • Failed Transactions: If money is debited from Your bank account/card but the transaction fails to complete on the Platform, the debited amount will be automatically credited back to your original payment source.
  • Duplicate Debits: In case of duplicate debits for a single order due to network timeouts, the extra debited amount will be auto-refunded.

7. Refund Processing Timelines & Mode of Refund

Refunds approved by KAMALO or payment partners are processed back to the original source of payment within the standard industry timelines set out below:

Payment MethodRefund Processing Timeline
UPI (VPA)24 to 48 Hours
Credit Card / Debit Card5 to 7 Business Days
Net Banking3 to 5 Business Days
KAMALO Coins / Promo CashbackInstant (Re-credited to app wallet/rewards log)

Note: Actual credit timelines may depend on your issuing bank's operational cycles.

8. Reversal of KAMALO Coins & Promotional Cashback

  • If a transaction that earned KAMALO Coins or cashback is subsequently cancelled or refunded, the corresponding KAMALO Coins or cashback credited for that transaction will be automatically deducted from Your account balance.
  • If Your KAMALO Coin balance is insufficient for deduction, your account may reflect a negative coin balance, or the equivalent cash amount may be adjusted against future refunds or payouts.

9. Chargebacks & Disputes

If You initiate an unauthorized chargeback with your card issuing bank without attempting resolution through KAMALO customer support first, KAMALO reserves the right to temporarily suspend your account and freeze pending rewards pending investigation.
