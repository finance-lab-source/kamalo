1. Purpose & Commitment

Kamal Intellect Private Limited is committed to providing a transparent, efficient, and fair customer support and grievance redressal framework for KAMALO users. This policy is aligned with:

  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT Rules 2011;
  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines on customer protection for digital payment transactions;
  • Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020; and
  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. First-Level Customer Support Channels

If You experience an issue with a transaction, reward credit, account access, or merchant voucher, You can reach our Level-1 Customer Support team through:

  • In-App Help & Support: Navigate to "Profile" -> "Help & Support" -> "Raise a Ticket" in the KAMALO app;
  • Email Support: Send an email detailing your issue and Transaction ID to kamaloapp09@gmail.com;
  • Website Contact Form: Available at https://kamalo.app.
  • First Response SLA: Acknowledgement within 24 hours.
  • Target Resolution SLA: Within 3 to 7 business days.

3. Escalation Matrix & Designated Grievance Officer Details

If your query is not resolved within the specified SLA, or if you are dissatisfied with the response provided by Level-1 Support, You may escalate the matter to our Grievance Officer.

4. Complaint Filing Procedure

When filing a formal grievance, please include:

  • Full Registered Name and Mobile Number;
  • Transaction Reference ID / Order ID (if applicable);
  • Clear summary of the issue or complaint;
  • Supporting screenshots or bank transaction statements (redact sensitive PINs/passwords);
  • Reference ID of the initial Level-1 support ticket.

5. Acknowledgement & Resolution Timelines

  • Acknowledgement: Every formal grievance received by the Grievance Officer will be acknowledged within 24 hours with a unique Ticket Tracking Number.
  • Redressal Window: The Grievance Officer shall investigate and resolve the complaint within 15 business days from the date of receipt, as mandated under applicable law.

6. Unresolved Disputes & Regulatory Escalation

If your complaint relates to a payment transaction, digital wallet, or UPI issue processed through our partner banks and remains unresolved after 30 days, You may escalate the matter to the Reserve Bank of India's Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme at https://cms.rbi.org.in.

For general consumer protection complaints, users may access the National Consumer Helpline (NCH) portal or relevant judicial forums in Mumbai, Maharashtra.
