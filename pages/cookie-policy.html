1. What Are Cookies & Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device by websites, apps, and online services. Web beacons, SDKs, local storage, and pixels perform similar functions. This Policy explains how Kamal Intellect Private Limited uses these tracking technologies on https://kamalo.app and the KAMALO mobile app.

2. How KAMALO Uses Cookies, SDKs & Pixels

We use cookies and SDKs to:

  • Verify your identity and keep you securely logged in;
  • Remember your settings, preferences, and location;
  • Facilitate secure payment gateway routing;
  • Measure app performance, crash analytics, and server load;
  • Deliver targeted offers and evaluate marketing campaigns.

3. Categories of Cookies Used

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the Platform. They enable core functions such as network security, session management, user login, and fraud detection. You cannot opt out of these cookies as the Platform cannot function properly without them.

3.2 Functional & Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your choices (such as preferred language, city location, or display options) to provide a personalized experience.

3.3 Analytics & Performance Cookies

These cookies and SDKs collect aggregated, non-identifiable information about how users interact with the Platform (e.g., pages visited, session duration, app crashes). They help Us improve user experience and app architecture.

Examples: Google Analytics, Firebase SDK, Mixpanel.

3.4 Marketing & Measurement Cookies

These technologies track your activity across digital properties to display relevant merchant deals, cashback promotions, and sponsored offers on KAMALO and third-party ad networks.

4. Third-Party Cookies & Analytics Partners

Third-party service providers (such as payment processors, analytics engines, and advertising partners) may place cookies or SDKs on your device when you use the Platform. Third parties operate under their respective privacy and cookie policies.

5. Managing & Disabling Cookies

  • Browser Settings: You can configure your desktop or mobile browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to block, decline, or delete cookies.
  • App Permissions: On Android and iOS devices, You can manage tracking permissions, location access, and advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) via system settings.
  • Impact of Disabling: Disabling cookies or storage features may degrade your user experience, prevent login session persistence, or disable certain promotional rewards.

6. Consent & Policy Updates

By continuing to browse https://kamalo.app or using the KAMALO mobile app, You consent to our use of cookies and tracking technologies as described in this policy. We may update this policy periodically to reflect technology changes or statutory mandates.
