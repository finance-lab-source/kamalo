Got questions?

What is KAMALO?

KAMALO is India's Transactionverse: one ecosystem for shopping, payments, rewards and business opportunities.

Is KAMALO free to download?

Joining the waiting list is free. Details will be shared at launch.

How can I earn rewards?

Eligible transactions on participating services can unlock savings, KAMALO Coins and other rewards, subject to programme terms.

What are KAMALO Coins?

Reward units you can earn on eligible activity and redeem on qualifying purchases. Earning, redemption and expiry follow applicable terms.

Do I need to change my shopping habits?

No. KAMALO is built around transactions you already make.

Can I earn referral rewards?

Qualifying referrals can unlock referral rewards, subject to programme terms.

Is KAMALO only for individuals?

No. Startups, shops, manufacturers, service providers and established brands can join too.

Can I list my business?

Yes. Join the waiting list as a business and we'll reach out as onboarding opens.

When will KAMALO launch?

Launch details will be shared with waiting-list members first.
